---
title: "Paul Christiano on Losing Control of Superintelligence"
date: 2026-09-10
---

Paul Christiano, founder of the Alignment Research Center, former head of OpenAI’s language-model alignment team, and Senior Technical Advisor at NIST’s Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI), writing on X about joining the OpenAI Foundation Board and its Safety and Security Committee:

> If we build superintelligence without more robust alignment I expect we will permanently lose control of it. If that happens then most people could die.
