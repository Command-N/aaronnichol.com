Utterly delightful collection of weekly Milwaukee bus tickets from the 1950s. 

For an Australian comparison, the Powerhouse Collection contains NSW bus tickets from [1966-1970](https://collection.powerhouse.com.au/object/364067), which are primitive in comparison. The [weekly tickets](https://collection.powerhouse.com.au/object/364093) covering the period are just as utilitarian.

I also stumbled on a 358 page(!) [tome](https://ozrevenues.com/tickets/FP-Vol1.pdf) documenting paper bus tickets in country NSW. 

Via [John Gruber](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/04/12/golden-tickets)
