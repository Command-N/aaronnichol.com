A little PSA to anyone in Australia currently subscribed to Claude Pro through the App Store. If you sign up for a Pro plan in the store it costs the same as signing up directly. Upgrading to Max is a different story.

Here’s my hypothesis: since Claude exploded onto the scene late last year, the vast majority of new paid subscribers have signed up in-app, and currently pay Anthropic via Apple or Google—and the vast majority of these paid subscribers are on the Pro plan. 

Within that group of Pro subscribers a subset already have or will eventually push the limits of their subscription. Claude tracks usage both in 5-hour session blocks and across a week. Breach either of these and you have three options: wait until the meter resets; give Anthropic a credit card and pay for extra usage at the going API rates until the meter resets (Mac only from what I've seen), or; upgrade to the Max plan in 5x and 20x flavours.

Let’s take each of these in turn.

Waiting until the meter resets is simple but disruptive when you’re in the middle of something. It’s a jarring experience to suddenly be forced to wait, and a consistent source of angst amongst Claude users on all tiers. 

Giving Anthropic your credit card and using the API until the meter resets is a relatively new option. I've only seen it in the Mac app, presumably because it would run afoul of Apple's in-app purchase restrictions. It’s an elegant option but you are giving your credit card information to Anthropic. This is a much more concrete form of ID than what you provide when you sign up in app. Some may not be comfortable with that given your privacy stance and how much personal information you give the LLM. 

Theres a trap here that most users aren’t even aware of if they’ve only ever subscribed via the app. 

A less obvious approach—and the one I use—is to keep a Pro sub for everyday queries and use the API for Claude Code. That is a story for another day. 

In Australia a monthly Claude Pro subscription is $34 whether you sign via the App Store or through Anthropic themselves. Max plans however are up to 26% more expensive through the App Store.

I first signed up for Claude Pro in-app through the App Store sometime  

Then I started using Claude Code for complex development work and very quickly hit the limits of the Pro plan. 5-hour sessions would be exhausted in minutes. And I get it, 

I looked at upgrading to Max 5x or 20x but the upgrade jump was huge. Max 5x at AUD $209, Max 20x at a whopping $429 per month. At those prices you pause and ask yourself how justifiable it is. 

Because my subscription is through the App Store, Anthropic obviously don’t show me the non-App-Store prices, and they direct me to the App Store even when I login to the website and try to upgrade—so I wasn’t even aware you can get cheaper Max plans directly through Anthropic. It doesn’t help that Anthropic don’t publish Australian subscription prices for Max plans on their website. You need to start the sign up process and give them an Australian phone number before you can see them. 

To save any fellow Aussies the hassle, here they are:

As you can see, Anthropic is effectively wearing the cost of Apple’s commission on the Pro plan but absolutely are not for the Max plans. This makes sense, 30% of 30-odd dollars isn’t devastating but 30% of $300 is.

The real value for money Claude plan is Max 20x—which for me is an AUD $430 per month commitment.
